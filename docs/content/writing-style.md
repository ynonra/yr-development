# Writing style: how Yinon writes

Reference for anything written in Yinon's voice: blog posts, project case studies, site copy, social posts. Use it when writing new content, and when reviewing content someone else (or an AI) wrote.

Derived from Yinon's own writing on this site: the original homepage, the project case studies in `data/projects.js` (the ones Yinon wrote, not the two added in 2026), the thank-you page, and lines Yinon wrote during the 2026 redesign (for example "מבטיח לא להציק. הרבה").

## The voice in one line

A friendly developer from Karmiel talking to you at eye level: first person, warm, a little cheeky, honest about the hard parts, and always concrete.

## Who talks to whom

- **Yinon speaks in first person singular.** "אני מפתח", "פיתחתי", "הצעתי ללקוח". Never the corporate "אנו מציעים ללקוחותינו". That line existed in an old meta description, and it's exactly what to avoid.
- **The reader is "אתם"** (plural, so it works for everyone): "בטח גם לכם יש חלום...", "מוזמנים לדבר איתי".
- **Clients are real people with real problems**: "הלקוח פנה אלי לאחר שחווה אכזבה ממפתח קודם".

## Signature moves

Each comes with a real example. Use them; don't overuse them.

| Move | What it looks like | How often |
|---|---|---|
| Warm, casual opener | "אהלן, אני ינון." | Once, at the start of personal pieces |
| The dream motif | "אם אתם יכולים לחלום את זה, אני יכול ליצור את זה", "אפשר לומר שאני מגשים להם חלומות", "החלום רוקם עור וגידים" | It's the brand's thread; one touch per piece is enough |
| A joke in parentheses | "(שזה היה ממש קשה אבל מחשל :) )", "(כי בשביל זה יש מחשבים, לא?)", "(רגע של כנות...)" | One or two per article |
| Honest about effort and limits | "לוקח קצת זמן, אבל שווה לחכות!", "מהר זה טוב רק כשזה גם עובד :)" | Whenever promising speed or results |
| A "before" the reader recognizes | "כל ניהול ההזמנות... התבצע בצורה ידנית... באמצעות קבצי אקסל, התכתבויות ב-WhatsApp, ובעל פה. אתם יכולים לנחש שהתנהלות כזו הובילה ל..." | Core of every case study and many articles |
| Rhetorical question, then an invitation | "יש לכם רעיון מגניב שעתיד לשנות לאנשים את החיים לטובה? דברו אליי." | Section endings, CTAs |
| A deadpan twist at the end | "מבטיח לא להציק. הרבה", "החליפה רק לתמונה ;)" | Short lines only: CTAs, captions |
| Ellipsis for comic timing | "והתוצאה?... בואו לראות בעצמכם...", "והיד עוד נטויה…" | Sparingly |
| Short, upbeat closers | "לחיים!", "בהצלחה!", "מוזמנים לצחוק קצת גם אתם!" | End of a story |
| Smileys | ":)" and ";)" at the end of a sentence | At most one per section; never two in a row |

## Register

- **Mostly everyday Hebrew**: מגניב, תותחים, ללכלך את הידיים, בונוס מטורף על חשבון הבית, ממש.
- **Plus a pinch of literary or Torah-world phrasing**, which is part of Yinon's background: "על כן", "מחד... ומאידך", "על אחת כמה וכמה", "רוקם עור וגידים", "והיד עוד נטויה". One or two per piece adds character; more sounds stiff.
- **The background is a feature**: Karmiel, the north, yeshiva study, reading the Torah portion, building for religious institutions and nonprofits. Mention it when it's relevant to the reader, for example "בתור תלמיד ישיבה, ספר זה הוא חובה...". Don't make it a gimmick.
- **Tech terms stay in English, with a plain-Hebrew explanation the first time**: "PWA, מה שאומר שהאפליקציה עובדת גם מחנויות האפליקציות וגם כאתר רגיל".

## How pieces are built

**Case studies** (the project pages) follow a fixed arc:

1. **הצורך של הלקוח**: who they are and what hurt.
2. **תהליך הפיתוח**: what was built and with what, including the honest bits ("היה מאתגר מצד אחד... ומצד שני...").
3. **השפעת המוצר**: what changed, in concrete terms ("מאז שהאתר עלה לאוויר, תלמידים רבים מוצאים אותו בחיפוש בגוגל").

**Blog posts** answer a search query, so the order changes. The voice stays the same.

1. **Answer first.** The opening paragraph answers the title's question directly, with a number or a clear verdict. This is what Google snippets and AI assistants quote.
2. **Then the "it depends", made concrete**: the factors, real ranges, and a table when comparing options.
3. **A story from a real project** as proof (see the projects below). Never invent clients or results.
4. **Mistakes to avoid**, said candidly.
5. **Short FAQ** (3-5 questions phrased the way people search).
6. **Personal close plus CTA**: one line in Yinon's voice and an invitation to talk.

## Don'ts

- **Corporate plural or brochure-speak**: "אנו מציעים פתרונות טכנולוגיים מתקדמים", "חדשנות", "מצוינות", "מענה הוליסטי".
- **Hype without proof**: every claim gets a number, an example or a project.
- **Inventing anything**: clients, quotes, results, prices. If a number is a market range, say so.
- **Talking down to readers or competitors.** Being candid about bad practices is fine; naming and shaming is not.
- **Walls of text.** Paragraphs of 2-4 sentences, then a break.
- **Too many jokes.** The humor works because the content is solid. One wink per section, max.

## Mechanics

- **Spelling.** The original drafts have informal spellings (פרוייקט, איפיון, מדוייק, סיפריית, ישומון). In published copy use the standard forms: פרויקט, אפיון, מדויק, ספריית, יישומון. Same voice, more credible, and it matches what people type into Google.
- **No space before "!" or "?"** ("בהצלחה!", not "בהצלחה !").
- **Hebrew punctuation**: geresh and gershayim (וכו׳, צ׳אטבוט, בד״ץ). Hyphen after a prefix on a Latin word: ב-Shopify, ה-AI, ל-ChatGPT.
- **Money**: ₪ before the number, as on the site (₪3,500), with ranges written "₪3,500-₪8,000". Say whether VAT is included when it matters.
- **Numbers**: digits for anything measurable ("3 שבועות", "24/7").
- **Dashes**: prefer a period or a comma over long dashes.

## Cover image

Every post gets an illustrated cover in the site's style: the palette, white floating "chips" like the hero's, and at most one handwritten note. It shows a scene from the article, not its title (the title is always next to it).

1. Copy a template in `scripts/blog-covers/` to `<slug>.html` and change the scene.
2. Render it: `node scripts/blog-covers/render.mjs <slug>` (run from a folder with Playwright and WebKit installed). This writes `public/images/blog/<slug>.webp` for the site and `<slug>-og.jpg` for link previews.
3. Add `coverAlt` to the front matter: a short Hebrew description starting with "איור:".

The blog picks the cover up by slug. Dates are shown automatically: the Hebrew date first, the Gregorian one secondary.

## Checklist before publishing

- [ ] First person singular, reader as "אתם".
- [ ] The first paragraph answers the title.
- [ ] At least one real project or concrete example, linked.
- [ ] Numbers are real or clearly labelled as market ranges.
- [ ] One or two parenthetical jokes or winks, not more.
- [ ] Standard spelling; no space before ! or ?.
- [ ] Ends with a personal line and an invitation to talk.
- [ ] Has a cover and `coverAlt`; links only to posts published before it.
